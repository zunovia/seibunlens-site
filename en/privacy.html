SeibunLens Privacy Policy (English reference translation)

Established: October 24, 2026 (Japanese version)

Revised: November 3, 2026 (added memos for each ingredient)

This is a reference translation. The Japanese version (https://zunovia.github.io/seibunlens-site/privacy.html ) is the official version, and if there is any difference, the Japanese version prevails.

Sur Communication, a general incorporated association in Japan (the "Operator"), sets out how information is handled in the app "SeibunLens" (the "App") as follows.

1. Operator and contact

  • Operator: Sur Communication (general incorporated association, Japan)
  • Contact point for inquiries, complaints and consultations: info@surc.online
  • We will answer without delay with our address and the name of our representative if you ask us.

2. Information the App does not collect

The App does not collect names, contact details, accounts, device identifiers, advertising IDs or location (the information that Google ML Kit, used by the App, sends to Google is described in section 6).

Photos and the full text read from photos are never sent outside your device.

3. Information handled only on your device (it does not reach the Operator)

  • Photos you take or choose, the text read, the reading results and the results you corrected
  • The App's settings and records of your consent
  • Ingredient names you add to "My list"
  • Memos for each ingredient that you write on the ingredient explanation screen or the history screen (you can edit or delete them at any time)
  • "Saved results (history)": the ingredient list, the text read and the ingredient memos at that time, when you tap "Save" on the result screen (photos are not stored; you can delete them at any time on the history screen)

Text and barcodes are read on your device (Google ML Kit; the reading models are included in the App).

Photos taken from the App (the device's camera app takes the photo and saves it in a place reserved for the App) are deleted when reading is finished, and any that remain are deleted after one day. Photos chosen with the photo picker are only read where they are; the App neither deletes nor copies them.

The App's information is not included in device backups or transfers to a new device.

If you tap "Search the web" on an ingredient's explanation screen, your device's browser opens Google search results. The ingredient name (and words such as "cosmetic ingredient") is then sent by your browser to Google LLC. It is not sent by the App, and Google's privacy policy applies. Nothing is sent unless you tap it.

If you tap "Download" on the history screen, a file containing the ingredient list (including ingredient memos) and the text read is saved to a location you choose. If you tap "Share", the same content is passed to an app you choose. Both happen only by your action and are not sent to the Operator by the App. How the content is handled after that is governed by the app or service you chose.

  • Anything that could not be sent is kept on your device and sent again the next time you open the App. If it still cannot be sent after 7 days, it is discarded.
  • You provide product data under CC0 (Terms of Use, Article 5).

5. Destination and information received through communication

  • Feedback and product data are sent to the Operator's receiving server. The receiving server runs on the services of Cloudflare, Inc. (Cloudflare Workers, KV and D1), and the data is stored on that company's facilities.
  • Because of how the internet works, Cloudflare receives connection records such as your IP address.
  • To prevent abuse, the Operator's receiving server converts IP addresses into a salted hash (a form that cannot be reversed) to count the number of sends per hour, and that hash is deleted automatically after two hours. The IP address itself is not stored. The receiving server is also set not to store request logs.

6. Information sent by Google ML Kit

Google ML Kit, used by the App, does not send photos or the text read, but sends the following information to Google LLC for diagnostics and improvement (Google's description: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure ).

  • Device information (manufacturer, model, OS version, available machine learning hardware)
  • App information (package name, version)
  • A per-installation identifier (Google states that it is not intended to uniquely identify a user or device)
  • Processing time, image format and size, input and output size, error types, etc.

Google states that it does not pass this information to third parties.

7. List of information the App sends

You can check what the App sends, where and why at any time under "What this app sends" on the App's first screen and in "Settings" (the same contents as sections 4 to 6 above).

8. Security measures

  • Communication with the receiving server is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Only the Operator holds the administrative key of the receiving server, limiting who can handle the information sent.
  • Information past the retention periods above is deleted.

9. Age

The App is intended for people aged 13 and over. If you are under 18, get your parent's or guardian's consent before sending product data.

10. Changes

When this Policy changes, we will announce it in the App or on this page. If we expand the information sent or the purposes of use, we will ask for your consent again.
